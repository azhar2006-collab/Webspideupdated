Privacy Policy

At Webspide, your privacy is a top priority for us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share your personal information when you interact with our website, webspide.com, and our services.

Information We Collect

When you visit our website, certain non-personal information is automatically collected through your browser or device. This includes your IP address, browser type and version, operating system, device type, pages visited, referring and exit pages, and approximate location. This information helps us understand how visitors use our site, allowing us to improve performance, content, and usability.

We may also collect personal information that you voluntarily provide through forms, email, live chat, or direct communication. This includes your name, email address, phone number, company name, and specific details related to your project or service inquiries. This information is used solely for communication and to provide the services or support you request.

If you choose to make a payment for our services, payment processing is handled securely by third-party providers such as PayPal, Stripe, or direct bank transfer. Webspide does not store sensitive financial information like credit card numbers. You should refer to the respective payment provider's privacy policy for more details about how your payment data is handled.

Use of Information

We use the information collected to communicate with you, understand your needs, and provide the services you've requested. It also helps us enhance our website, personalize your experience, and send relevant updates or marketing communications. You can unsubscribe from promotional emails at any time by following the opt-out link provided in the message.

Data Security

Webspide takes reasonable precautions to safeguard your data. We implement industry-standard measures such as secure servers, restricted access, and data encryption where applicable to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, or misuse.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies to enhance functionality and analyze traffic. Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help us understand how users interact with our site. These cookies do not personally identify you unless you choose to provide such information. You may choose to disable cookies through your browser settings, although doing so may affect certain features on the website.

Third-Party Links and Integrations

Our website may include links to third-party sites or services, such as social media platforms, chat tools like Tawk.to, or payment gateways. These websites have their own privacy policies, which you should review. Webspide is not responsible for the content, practices, or data handling of external sites.

Sharing of Information

Webspide does not sell or rent your personal data to third parties. We may share information in the following limited cases:

  • With trusted partners working under confidentiality agreements, solely to support our operations.
  • When required by law, subpoena, or legal process.
  • To prevent or investigate fraud, security issues, or harm to others.
  • To enforce our Terms of Service or protect our legal rights.

International Data Transfers

If you are accessing our services from outside India, please note that your data may be transferred to and processed in India or other countries where we or our service providers operate. By using our services, you consent to this transfer, and we take steps to ensure your data is handled under applicable data protection laws.

Email Communications

By contacting us or subscribing to our newsletter, you agree to receive service-related or promotional emails. You can opt out at any time by using the unsubscribe link found in our emails. We do not send unsolicited marketing content without your permission.

Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, regulatory, or operational reasons. The updated version will always be available on this page with the revised effective date. Continued use of our website or services after any such changes implies your acceptance of the updated policy.
