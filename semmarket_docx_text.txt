Google Search Ads – FAQ

How do you conduct keyword and competitor research?

We identify high-intent commercial keywords, long-tail opportunities, customer terminology, search demand, and irrelevant searches. Competitor advertisements, offers, landing pages, positioning, and visible keyword themes are reviewed to identify opportunities without copying strategies that may not suit your business.

How do you create and optimize search advertisements?

Our team writes clear headlines and descriptions around customer intent, business benefits, differentiators, offers, and calls to action. Multiple advertisement variations are created and tested so reliable campaign data can guide future messaging and creative decisions.

How are bids and advertising budgets managed?

We select bidding approaches based on campaign objectives, conversion data, account maturity, available budget, and commercial priorities. Bids and budgets are reviewed regularly to control acquisition costs and support stronger-performing keywords, advertisements, devices, locations, and markets.

How do you target relevant audiences?

Campaigns can use keywords, locations, languages, devices, schedules, audience signals, remarketing, and approved customer data. Targeting is continuously adjusted according to campaign performance, market conditions, customer behaviour, and evolving business priorities.

Which Google Ads assets do you configure?

Suitable assets may include sitelinks, callouts, structured snippets, calls, locations, prices, promotions, images, and lead forms. Assets are selected according to the business, campaign objective, customer journey, platform eligibility, and available information.

Do you optimize PPC landing pages?

We review landing-page relevance, messaging, mobile responsiveness, loading speed, trust elements, forms, navigation, and calls to action. Recommendations are designed to reduce friction between the advertisement, customer expectations, and the intended conversion.

How do you conduct A/B testing?

We test selected variables such as headlines, descriptions, offers, audiences, landing-page sections, and calls to action. Changes are measured carefully so results remain interpretable and can support informed campaign improvements.

How do negative keywords reduce wasted advertising spend?

Negative keywords prevent advertisements from appearing for irrelevant, unsuitable, or low-value searches. Search-term reports are reviewed regularly to identify new exclusions that protect advertising budgets, campaign relevance, click quality, and conversion performance.

Google Shopping Ads – FAQ

What information appears in Google Shopping Ads?

Shopping advertisements may display a product image, product name, price, retailer, availability, promotions, and other relevant information. The details are supplied through approved product feeds and their associated ecommerce landing pages.

How do you configure Google Merchant Center?

We review account setup, business information, website verification, shipping, returns, tax settings, product sources, feed connections, and policy requirements. Potential issues are addressed before campaigns are activated wherever possible.

How do Shopping Ads attract high-intent customers?

Shopping campaigns appear for relevant product searches and allow users to review visual, pricing, and retailer information before clicking. This can attract visitors who are already comparing products and considering a purchase.

How do you manage Shopping campaign bids?

Bidding is selected according to revenue, conversion value, available data, product margins, campaign maturity, and advertising budget. Performance is reviewed at campaign, product-group, category, brand, and individual-product levels where appropriate.

How are products organized within campaigns?

Products can be grouped according to category, brand, product type, custom labels, margin, seasonality, inventory, or commercial priority. This structure supports clearer budgeting, bidding, reporting, and product-level performance analysis.

Do you provide dynamic remarketing support?

Where suitable, dynamic remarketing can reconnect with previous visitors using advertisements related to products they viewed. Campaigns are coordinated with audience data, product feeds, conversion tracking, consent requirements, and applicable privacy practices.

Can you manage Local Inventory Ads?

For eligible retailers with physical locations, accurate product and store data can support advertisements showing nearby inventory. Implementation depends on business eligibility, reliable local feeds, store information, and current platform requirements.

How do you resolve product-feed issues?

We monitor missing attributes, incorrect values, mismatched pricing, unavailable products, image concerns, identifier problems, and policy disapprovals. Feed corrections are coordinated with ecommerce, development, inventory, and product-data teams.

How is Shopping campaign performance measured?

We track impressions, clicks, conversions, revenue, advertising costs, return on advertising spend, product performance, feed health, and acquisition trends. Insights guide bidding, budgeting, product promotion, feed improvements, and campaign priorities.
